About Al Amwaj Water Innovation

Engineering Water. Empowering Progress.

Established in 2007, Al Amwaj Water Innovation delivers integrated water engineering solutions designed around performance, precision, and long-term reliability.

Our expertise spans swimming pool systems, water treatment and RO plants, water softening, pumping and boosting systems, fountains, and fire protection solutions.

We combine technical expertise with a commitment to quality, operational efficiency, and lasting client relationships. Every project reflects our focus on dependable engineering, practical innovation, and solutions built to perform.
